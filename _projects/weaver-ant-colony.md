A founding colony of weaver ants (<i>Oecophylla smaragdina</i>): a single queen and about seventeen workers, housed in a test tube kept in a dark, cool cupboard. At about thirty workers, the colony was moved to a plastic tub outdoors, and the queen died within one night.

The collapse later became the starting observation for the essay [“Ants in the Heat of Survival”](/writings/papers/ants-in-the-heat-of-survival/).
