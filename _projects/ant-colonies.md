## Messor: harvester ants

### *Messor cephalotes*
One queen, 6 workers and brood.

### *Messor structor*
One queen, 70 workers and brood.

### *Messor barbarus*
One queen, 15 workers and brood.

## Camponotus: carpenter ants

### *Camponotus albosparsus*
One queen, 1 worker and brood.

### *Camponotus parius*
One queen, 9 workers and brood.

### *Camponotus fedtschenkoi*
One queen, 1 worker and brood.

## Carebara: marauder ants

### *Carebara diversa* (colony 1)
One queen, 40 workers and brood.

### *Carebara diversa* (colony 2)
One queen, about 200 workers (including 12 super majors) and brood.
