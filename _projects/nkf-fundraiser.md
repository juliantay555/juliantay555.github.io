An orchestral fundraiser for the National Kidney Foundation: organised, led and performed. Against a goal of US$15,000, the concert raised over US$45,500 for kidney failure patients. A separate effort raised more than US$6,500 for the Singapore Peritoneal Dialysis Educational Forum.

Next step: a meeting with the foundation's sustainability office on further contributions.
